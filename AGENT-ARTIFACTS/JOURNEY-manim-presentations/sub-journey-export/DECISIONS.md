- 2026-10-30 probe (probes/seeked_probe*.py; CI runs 41175396144, 41175537219, 41175725626 on macos-latest, headless Playwright 1.64.1 WebKit and stock Chrome; Chrome also local): re-verification of the paused-seek path, as the brief asks.
  - WebKit: after a `<video>` has played, or before it has fired `loadeddata`, the first paused seek fires `seeked` with the right `currentTime` but leaves the previous frame on screen and on canvas (probe 1: target 59 read 0; probe 2: play→pause then seek 59 read 0, screenshot agrees). A never-played element after `loadeddata` seeks correctly every time, on screen and on canvas. Chrome: every case correct.
  - So the P5 rule "a video it seeks while paused swaps in on `seeked`" holds only for a fresh element. The player therefore makes **one fresh `<video>` per show**: it is inserted under the front video, waits for `loadeddata`, seeks paused to (k + 0.5)/fps, is raised on `seeked` (the old element is then removed and released), and then plays if the show plays. Probe 3 ran exactly this route through 12 steps (Before, Next, Next onto a loop, Previous to a last frame, Replay, hold of the front beat, more Previous/Next): canvas and screenshot equal the target frame at every swap, in both engines; one element left at the end.
  - Every show is a paused seek, so the player has one swap route (`seeked`) and never uses `requestVideoFrameCallback`. This replaces the NORTH-STAR's "held until the incoming requestVideoFrameCallback" and P4's "Next swaps on its rVFC" (WebKit never fires rVFC after a paused seek, DECISIONS P5) with "held until the incoming fresh element's `seeked`". Next still presents frame 0 before the old video hides, then plays.
  - Blob URLs keep the plan's ±1 window (created on entering it, revoked on leaving it, after the swap so the front element never loses its source). Live media elements are at most two (front + incoming), below the plan's ±1 count of three.
  - Measured transition (show call to swap): Chrome 14–25 ms on the runner, 4–6 ms local; WebKit 113–990 ms on the 3-vCPU runner VM.
