Mechanism discovered (adopt in clip.py / manim_hooks.py):
- Exact fast-forward = for plays with num_plays < from_animation_number, override `renderer.update_skipping_status` to leave `skip_animations=False`, and null all IO for that play: `camera.capture_mobjects`, `camera.reset`, `camera.set_frame_to_background`, `renderer.get_frame` no-ops; `file_writer.add_partial_movie_file(None)`, `begin_animation(False, …)`, `end_animation(False)`, `write_frame` no-op. Manim's own play_internal then runs unmodified (per-frame update_to_time, time advance in add_frame, stop_condition, closing update_mobjects(0), frozen-wait arithmetic) — exact by construction, no re-implementation. Partial-file indices stay aligned (None entries).
- TeeContext static-image side map: the frame that becomes `static_image` is the snapshot taken inside `renderer.save_static_frame_data`; wrap that method and promote the last snapshot `(array, digest)` to the side map. ("Latest get_frame" is wrong: every rendered frame calls get_frame.)
